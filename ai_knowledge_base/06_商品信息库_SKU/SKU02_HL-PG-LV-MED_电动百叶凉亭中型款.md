## 客户常见问题

**Q: 中型款值得多花 5% 吗?**
A: For projects in slightly higher wind areas (coastal Europe, Australian coast) or long-term commercial use (hotels, restaurants), the additional 0.2mm aluminum wall and reinforced louver provide better fatigue resistance over 5-10 years. For typical home use, the standard model is sufficient.

**Q: 重型款比中型款贵多少?**
A: Heavy-duty model (HL-PG-LV-HD) is approximately 35% higher than medium model. Heavy-duty is recommended for spans >5m or high snow load (Canada, Alps, Nordic).
