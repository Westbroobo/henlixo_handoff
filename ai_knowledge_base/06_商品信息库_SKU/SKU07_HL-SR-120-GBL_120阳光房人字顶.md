## 客户常见问题

**Q: 5+5 夹胶玻璃和单层钢化的区别?**
A: 5+5 laminated glass = two 5mm tempered glass panels bonded with PVB interlayer. If broken, the PVB holds glass fragments in place — critical for overhead glass roofs to prevent falling shards. EN 12150 certified. Single-layer tempered glass shatters into pebbles when broken.

**Q: 阳光房和凉亭区别?**
A: A sunroom is a fully enclosed glass/aluminum room — used like an indoor space with natural light. A pergola is an open or semi-open outdoor structure for shade. Sunrooms are usually permanent extensions; pergolas can be relatively standalone.

**Q: 屋顶玻璃需要清洗吗?**
A: Yes, annual cleaning is recommended. The smooth glass surface naturally sheds most dust during rain. For heavy environments (industrial, dusty), 2x per year is recommended.
