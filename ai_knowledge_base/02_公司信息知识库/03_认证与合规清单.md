## 客户问起其它认证 — 必须的标准回答

### 第一档:不能直接说"有"

| 客户问 | AI 标准回答 |
|--------|------------|
| Do you have SGS report? | SGS testing can be arranged for your specific order. Could you share which parameters need to be tested? (e.g. salt spray, wind load, glass impact) |
| Do you have ASTM compliance? | Specific ASTM standards depend on the project type. Please share which ASTM standard your project requires, and we will confirm with our technical team. |
| Do you have AS/NZS certification? | For the Australian market, we can configure the product to meet relevant AS/NZS standards (e.g. AS 1170 wind load, AS 1926 pool fencing). Please share the project specs and we'll confirm. |
| Do you have UKCA? | UKCA marking shares the same technical basis as CE. We can arrange UKCA documentation for UK shipments. |
| Do you have ISO 9001? | Our internal QMS follows ISO 9001 principles. The latest ISO 9001 certificate status can be confirmed for your project upon request. |
| Do you have TUV? | TUV third-party testing can be arranged for specific orders. Please share the testing scope your project requires. |
| Do you have EN 1090? | EN 1090 applies to structural steel and aluminum components. For projects requiring EN 1090, we can arrange testing/certification on a per-order basis. |
| Do you have EN 13561? | EN 13561 applies to external blinds and awnings. For pergola/canopy projects requiring EN 13561, we can arrange a notified-body wind load classification test. |
| Do you have AAMA / RoHS? | We can provide material declarations and arrange testing if required by your project. |

### 第二档:绝对不能瞎承诺

- ❌ 不可以说"我们所有产品都通过 CE / SGS / ASTM"
- ❌ 不可以说"一张 CE 证书全球通用"
- ❌ 不可以把"自我声明 DoC"当成"完整 CE DoP"
- ❌ 不可以承诺电机自带 UL / CSA / PSE / KC(电机国别标准要客户指定品牌后才能确认)
- ❌ 不可以承诺 CBAM 碳数据已就绪(2026 年欧盟 CBAM 全面强制,需逐项核算)

## 按目的市场列出的强制合规要求(供 AI 提醒客户)

### 欧盟 EU (含英国 UK)
- CE 必备(机械指令/低压指令/EMC),UK 加 UKCA
- 户外遮阳产品适用 EN 13561(需公告机构 NB 介入风载分级)
- 玻璃需符合 EN 12150(已具备:5+5 夹胶玻璃)
- CBAM:2026 年起,铝制品需备隐含碳数据
- 电机:CE 低压指令 + EMC 指令

### 美国 USA
- 玻璃可能需要 ASTM E1300、ASTM E330(风载)、ASTM C1036(玻璃质量)
- 部分州 NFRC 标签(阳光房有玻璃)
- 电机:UL 或 ETL 认证(由电机品牌承担)
- 泳池围栏:IBC / 各州地方泳池法规

### 加拿大 Canada
- CSA 电气认证(电机)
- CCMC 评估(部分省份建筑产品)
- 雪载按 NBC 各省附录(BC、Ontario、Quebec 不同)

### 澳大利亚 / 新西兰 Australia / NZ
- AS 1170 风载、AS/NZS 1170.2
- AS 1926.1-2012 泳池围栏(护栏出口必查)
- 电机:SAA / RCM mark(电机品牌承担)
- 玻璃:AS/NZS 2208

### 中东 GCC (UAE / KSA / Qatar)
- SASO(沙特强制)
- GCC G-Mark(海湾通用)
- 高温/沙尘环境 — 推荐升级氟碳三涂 + 加密胶条

### 日本 Japan
- PSE(电机强制)
- JIS 玻璃标准

### 韩国 Korea  
- KC mark(电机强制)
- KS 标准(玻璃/铝合金)

## CBAM(欧盟碳关税)备忘 — 2026 年起重要

- 适用产品:铝制品(含 pergola、sunroom、railing 主体铝部件)
- 客户问起:"We are aware of the EU CBAM regulation starting 2026. For shipments to EU, we can provide embedded carbon data on a per-order basis. Please confirm if your project requires CBAM declaration."
- 不可承诺"我们碳数据已全部上传 CBAM Registry",除非已实际完成
