## 质量管理流程(8 道工序)

```
原材料入厂检验
   ↓
铝型材切割与编号
   ↓
CNC 加工 / 激光切割
   ↓
焊接 / 组装
   ↓
表面处理(粉末喷涂 / 氟碳三涂)
   ↓
试装与五金匹配检查
   ↓
电机 + 排水 + 防水测试
   ↓
出货前拍照 / 视频确认 + 包装 + 装柜
```

### 8 个质检节点的英文说法(可直接报客户)

```
Step 1 — Raw Material Inspection
        Aluminum profiles, glass, PC panels, motors and hardware inspected upon arrival.

Step 2 — Cutting & Labeling
        Profiles cut to project dimensions and labeled with order/SKU code.

Step 3 — CNC Processing & Laser Cutting
        Precision holes, slots and steel reinforcement parts machined.

Step 4 — Welding & Assembly
        Frames and louver modules assembled, dimensions verified.

Step 5 — Surface Treatment
        Powder coating or fluorocarbon 3-coat spray applied; coating thickness inspected.

Step 6 — Pre-Assembly Trial
        Complete unit trial-assembled to verify fit, square and component matching.

Step 7 — Functional Testing
        Motor operation, louver angles, drainage and water-tightness tested.

Step 8 — Photo/Video Confirmation, Packing & Loading
        Pre-shipment photo and video sent to customer; packed in plywood crate; loaded into container.
```

## 标准包装方案

| 内层 | 中层 | 外层 |
|------|------|------|
| EPE 珍珠棉 / 气泡膜 | 木方固定立柱 + 玻璃单独打木架 | 防水缠膜 + 托盘 / 夹板木箱(选配) |

- 玻璃件标"易碎/向上"中英双语标签
- 装柜底部铺木条,避免与柜底冷凝水接触
- 数码影像存证(发柜前拍照视频留档,出现争议可追溯)

## 装柜数据(常用)

- 4×3m 拆装版 Pergola:压缩至 0.8-0.9 CBM/套
- 20GP 装载:18-24 套(拆装后约 +30% 装载效率)
- 40HQ 装载:50-65 套
- 阳光房按 ㎡ 拼柜,40HQ 约 200-300 ㎡
- 护栏按米拼柜,40HQ 约 500-800 米
