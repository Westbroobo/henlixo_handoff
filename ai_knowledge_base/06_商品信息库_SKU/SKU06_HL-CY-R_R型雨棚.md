## 客户常见问题

**Q: PC 板 vs 玻璃顶哪个好?**
A: PC panel is lighter, impact-resistant (20× tempered glass), and more economical — recommended for entrance canopies. Laminated glass offers premium clarity and is better suited for visible high-end project entrances. Cost difference: glass adds 30-50%.

**Q: 弧形会不会容易积水?**
A: The curve is engineered to direct water flow toward the back/sides where eave drainage collects it. There is no flat zone for water accumulation.

**Q: 可以做电动开合的吗?**
A: R-shaped canopy is a fixed-roof structure, not motorized. For motorized rain protection at entrances, please consider our motorized louvered pergola (smaller-size, 2-post variant).
