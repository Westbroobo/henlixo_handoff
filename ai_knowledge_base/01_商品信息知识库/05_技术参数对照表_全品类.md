## 抗风/雪载等级说明(谨慎口径)

**不可以直接报"抗风 X 级 / X km/h"。** 必须按下述梯度回答:

| 客户问法 | 标准回答(EN) |
|---------|--------------|
| Can it resist strong wind? | Our standard pergola is designed for typical residential wind loads. For specific wind ratings, we need to confirm the size, post spacing and the installation region — then our engineer will recommend the right reinforcement configuration. |
| What's the maximum wind speed? | The maximum sustainable wind speed depends on the structure size and anchoring method. Could you share the installation country/region and project size? We will calculate based on the local wind load standard. |
| Snow load? | Standard configuration is rated for light snow load. For regions with snow load above 0.5 kN/㎡ (e.g., Canada, Alps, Nordic countries), we recommend the heavy-duty model with reinforced beams and additional posts. |
