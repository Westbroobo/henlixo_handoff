## 客户常见问题

**Q: 平顶不会积水吗?**
A: The "flat" roof actually has a concealed 1-2% slope engineered into the frame, directing water to internal gutters and downpipes. From outside it appears flat, but water flows efficiently. No pooling under normal conditions.

**Q: 可以做天窗吗?**
A: Yes, electric skylights (one or multiple sections that open for ventilation) are a popular option. Cost approx USD 600-1000 per skylight.

**Q: 屋顶可以加装太阳能板吗?**
A: Yes, the flat roof structure can accommodate solar panels installed on dedicated rails above the glass (not directly on glass). Please share solar panel specs and total weight for structural review.
