## English Version (Long, 250 words — for company profile section)

Henlixo is a Chinese manufacturer dedicated to aluminum outdoor structure solutions, headquartered in Foshan, Guangdong Province — the country's largest aluminum building materials industrial cluster, supplying over 60% of China's aluminum outdoor product exports.

Our flagship product lines cover:

- **Aluminum Pergolas** — motorized louvered (standard / medium / heavy duty), manual flip-louvered, and pointed-roof pavilions, designed for spans from 3 to 8+ meters.
- **Sunrooms & Glass Houses** — 120-series gable-roof and flat-roof aluminum sunrooms with 5+5mm tempered laminated glass.
- **Mobile Retractable Sunrooms** — motorized track systems with 5mm polycarbonate panels, ideal for restaurants, hotels and poolside enclosures.
- **Aluminum Railing Systems** — for villa gardens, pool fencing, balconies and hotel landscape projects.
- **Complete accessory range** — including LED lighting, rain & wind sensors, side screens, sliding glass doors, smart Wi-Fi control modules.

Our customers include building material importers, outdoor furniture distributors, hotel and resort project teams, restaurant and café fit-out contractors, villa garden landscapers and real estate developers. We have shipped to over 30 countries across Europe (UK, Germany, France, Italy), North America (USA, Canada), Oceania (Australia, New Zealand), the Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia) and Southeast Asia.

Full customization is supported across size, color, structure design and accessories. Standard lead time is 25-35 days after order confirmation and 50% deposit. MOQ starts from 1 set for samples and project orders. Export packing includes plywood wooden crates on request.
