## 重要限制(AI 必须澄清)

**防风帘不是阳光房,不能完全防雨**

- ✅ 可以防风
- ✅ 可以挡 70-80% 雨水
- ❌ 不能 100% 防雨(布料接缝和上下导轨会渗水)
- ❌ 不能保温到室内级别(布料不是隔热材料)
- ❌ 不能独立销售当主结构(必须依附凉亭/阳光房)

## 客户常见问题

**Q: 防风帘下雨能用吗?**
A: Yes for light/moderate rain protection (blocks 70-80%). For full waterproofing, glass sliding doors or PC folding panels are recommended. Windproof curtain is designed primarily for wind blocking, not full rain sealing.

**Q: 可以单独买防风帘吗?**
A: Windproof curtain is an accessory designed to attach to an existing pergola or sunroom frame. It's not a standalone enclosure. If you need a standalone protected space, please consider our mobile retractable sunroom (HL-MSR-PC).

**Q: 风感联动是怎么工作的?**
A: An optional wind sensor automatically retracts the curtain when wind exceeds a set threshold (typically 30-40 km/h), preventing damage to the curtain and motor. Recommended for unattended commercial installations.

**Q: 4 面都装还是 1-2 面?**
A: Common practice: 4-side coverage for full enclosure (winter use), 1-2 side coverage for prevailing wind direction. We can quote per-face — let us know which sides you want covered.
