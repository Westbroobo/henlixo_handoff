## 客户常见问题(本 SKU 专属)

**Q: 标准款和重型款有什么区别?**
A: Standard model is designed for 3-6m spans and typical residential wind loads with 2.0mm aluminum wall. Heavy-duty model has 2.5mm wall, reinforced louver, larger posts, and is designed for spans >5m or high wind/snow regions.

**Q: 4×4m 用几套电机和灯带?**
A: For 4×4m (16㎡), we recommend 1 motor + 1 LED strip as standard. Customers can upgrade to 2 motors + 2 LED for faster operation and brighter lighting.

**Q: 立柱可以做圆柱形吗?**
A: Standard is square 80×80mm or 100×100mm. Round columns are available as ODM (cost +15%, lead time +7 days).

**Q: 颜色可以做香槟金或玫瑰金吗?**
A: Yes, any RAL color is available. Champagne / rose gold can be matched via RAL — please share a color reference or RAL number.
