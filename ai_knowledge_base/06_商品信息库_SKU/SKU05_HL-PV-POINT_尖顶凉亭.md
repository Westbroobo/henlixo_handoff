## 客户常见问题

**Q: 尖顶有百叶吗?**
A: Pointed-roof pavilion uses a fixed pointed aluminum roof, not louvered. For louvered + decorative style, we can combine — please share the design intent.

**Q: 可以做木纹吗?**
A: Yes, wood-grain transfer print finish is popular for resort/landscape projects — coffee wood, walnut, teak available. Cost +5% vs solid color.

**Q: 排水怎么走?**
A: Pointed roof uses eave gutter on the perimeter with 1-2 downpipes routed through chosen corner posts.
