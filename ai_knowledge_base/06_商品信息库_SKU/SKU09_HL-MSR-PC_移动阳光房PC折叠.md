## 客户常见问题

**Q: 真的可以全年用吗?**
A: Yes. Closed configuration provides effective rain protection and wind blocking. With optional heater integration, the enclosed space can be used in winter (down to -10°C ambient with heater). Open configuration restores full outdoor experience in summer.

**Q: PC 板会变黄吗?**
A: Our PC panel has a UV-protective coating on the outer surface. Under normal outdoor exposure, color stability is rated for 10+ years. Eventually some yellowing occurs in tropical UV conditions — PC panels can be replaced individually without dismantling the structure.

**Q: 风大可以开吗?**
A: For wind speeds above 40 km/h (Beaufort 6), we recommend keeping the structure closed. The wing-shape anti-detach rail prevents accidental opening during gusts. For typhoon-prone regions, manual lock-down points are recommended.

**Q: 电费高吗?**
A: 24V low-voltage motor draws ~150W during operation (only when opening/closing). Daily operation typically uses <0.5 kWh — negligible.

**Q: 可以加投影 / 音响?**
A: Yes, we can pre-install brackets and conduit for projector, speakers, heaters, lighting and misting systems. Please share your AV/HVAC requirements during design phase.
