## 客户常见问题

**Q: 重型款抗风等级?**
A: Henlixo's heavy-duty model is engineered to withstand significantly higher wind loads than residential standard configurations. For specific wind rating per your project (km/h or kN/㎡), please share installation country, post anchoring conditions and structural drawings — our engineer will provide an engineered wind load assessment.

**Q: 雪载多少?**
A: Heavy-duty configuration is designed for snow load up to ~1.5 kN/㎡ in standard configuration, and can be further reinforced for higher loads with additional cross beams. Please confirm your local snow load value (per NBC, EN 1991-1-3, ASCE 7) for engineering review.

**Q: 100×100 vs 120×120 立柱怎么选?**
A: 100×100mm posts are standard for heavy-duty up to ~36㎡. For larger spans (>6×8m) or very high wind/snow zones, 120×120mm posts are recommended.

**Q: 项目订单有什么特别支持?**
A: For project orders (heavy-duty, ≥3 sets), Henlixo provides: (1) 3D rendering, (2) Structural engineering recommendation per local code, (3) Container loading optimization, (4) Multi-language installation video, (5) Dedicated WhatsApp project channel, (6) Spare parts kit at 5% of total parts.
