## 客户常见问题

**Q: 手动 vs 电动差多少钱?**
A: A manual flip-louver pergola is typically 25-30% lower in total cost than the equivalent motorized model (saving on motor, remote, sensors). The aluminum frame and louver structure are the same.

**Q: 手动是不是很费力?**
A: The hand crank is geared — even a 4×4m roof opens fully with about 10-15 turns, taking around 20-30 seconds. Suitable for adults and older children.

**Q: 可以以后升级成电动吗?**
A: Yes, the structure is electric-ready. Adding a motor kit later costs around USD 300-500 + on-site installation.
