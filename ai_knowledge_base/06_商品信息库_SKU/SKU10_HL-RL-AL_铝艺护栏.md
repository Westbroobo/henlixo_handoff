## 客户常见问题

**Q: 你们能做泳池围栏吗?**
A: Yes, Henlixo manufactures aluminum pool fencing. The configuration (height, spacing, self-closing hinge, lock) is adjusted to meet your country's pool safety regulations — e.g. AS 1926.1 for Australia, IBC for USA, BS 1722 for UK. Please share country and pool dimensions.

**Q: 可以配大门吗?**
A: Yes, we manufacture matching gates — single-leaf, double-leaf, sliding gate, automatic gate options. Gate pricing is calculated separately based on gate area and mechanism.

**Q: 安装难吗?**
A: Aluminum railing is modular — posts anchor to concrete with M10-M16 stainless steel bolts (template provided), then horizontal rails slot into post brackets and are secured with hidden fixings. Most local contractors can install with our drawings.
